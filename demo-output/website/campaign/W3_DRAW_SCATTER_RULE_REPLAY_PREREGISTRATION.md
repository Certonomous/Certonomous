## 1. What is being replayed

The rule: *a ladder increment may not be published as a **feature** without
draw-scatter evidence at the rung it turns on, or with the absence of that
evidence stated on its face.*

**Definitions fixed before counting:**

- **States a ladder feature** = asserts something about the **SHAPE** of a
  sequence of increments — a turn, an oscillation, a divergence, a trend in
  increment magnitudes, or monotonicity used as an argument. **Not** merely
  reporting rung values, a band, or a `conclusive: false` verdict. A record that
  says *"the fit returns p = 2.25 and we reject it"* does not fire; one that says
  *"its increments grow with refinement"* does.
- **Evidence at the deciding rung** = replicate mesh draws exist at the rung the
  feature turns on (not merely somewhere in the family).
- **WITHDRAW** = the feature has been measured against draw scatter and does not
  survive.
- **RESTATE** = the feature is unmeasured; the remedy is to state the absence on
  its face, not to remove the claim. **This is the rule's default remedy.**

## 2. The pre-registered predictions

- **P1 — a MINORITY of campaign records fire.** Most records report values,
  costs, gates or verdicts rather than asserting a shape.
- **P2 — of those that fire, the large majority are RESTATE, not WITHDRAW.** I
  predict **≥ 70% RESTATE**. Only families whose scatter has actually been
  measured can be withdrawn, and there are four such families in the whole lab.
- **P3 — the withdrawals are already done.** B-52 and Ahmed 25° were withdrawn
  today; I predict the replay finds **no more than two** further withdrawal
  candidates, and that both, if any, are the NACA 0012 / 4412 wings whose
  replicate families already exist.

**P2 is the number the chief named as deciding whether the rule is cheap
discipline or an expensive purge, and it is fixed here before the count.**

**Entry condition, from the proposal:** a rule that fires on **every** record or
on **none** fails and is not adopted.

## 3. Method, and the recount discipline applied to the replay itself

The deliverable is a **count**, and §7 of this family's guidelines says a count
is derived twice by different routes before it is committed. So:

- **Route A** — programmatic: enumerate candidate records by pattern over the
  campaign corpus and stored studies, then classify each by reading it.
- **Route B** — an independent search agent sweeping the same corpus without
  Route A's pattern list.
- **The two counts are reconciled and any disagreement is reported**, not
  silently resolved. A disagreement is information about the definition, which is
  the part of this replay most likely to be wrong.

## 4. What will NOT be claimed

- No record is amended, withdrawn or restated **by this replay**. It produces the
  counts and the classification; the chief rules on adoption and on what moves.
- The retrofit (12.8–21.7 core-min, 5 ladders) is **not** run and not proposed
  for running before the rule is ruled on.
- No claim that firing records are wrong. **RESTATE is not a defect finding** —
  it says a check was never made, not that the claim is false.
