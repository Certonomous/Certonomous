does not trigger it. Fix: use the same full, unfiltered slice-cell set the

measured Cp, pressure, or velocity value — confirmed by the checks now
running to completion and producing the numbers in the table above, which
were previously simply unavailable (crash, not a wrong pass).

## Operator-quoted 0.8897 vs. the published 0.8892 — reconciled, not a discrepancy

The task background quoted "~0.8897" as the corrected NACA 4412 peak Cp.
The currently published `validation_numbers.json` records
`naca4412_wing.global_max_cp = 0.8892004618710941`. Both numbers are real
and traceable, and both were re-derived directly against today's fresh
extraction:

- Raw wall pressure peak (unchanged either way): **p_max = 100.08748 m²/s²**
  (`body.vtp`, freshly extracted this pass; matches PROOF.md's earlier
  citation of "100.09").
- q_kinematic = 112.5 m²/s² (0.5 × 15² at U_inf = 15 m/s).
- **0.8897** = p_max / q = 100.08748 / 112.5 = 0.889666 → rounds to 0.8897.
  This is the figure in `sdk/tests/test_field_render.py:321` ("the two
  disagree by Cp 0.8897 against 0.8503"), `PROOF.md` ("Cp_max = 0.89"), and
  `REGENERATION_REPORT.txt` ("max = 100.09 m²/s² (Cp 0.889)"). It does
  **not** subtract the far-field reference pressure.
- **0.8892004618710941** = (p_max − p_inf) / q = (100.08748 − 0.05243) /
  112.5. This is the figure in `validation_numbers.json`
  (`naca4412_wing.global_max_cp`), independently reproduced today by
  re-running `validate_pressure_fields.py` against the fresh extraction —
  output matched to every printed digit, and the regenerated evidence PNG
  is pixel-identical to the one on disk.

Both artifacts are named explicitly above; neither has been altered to
match the other. The ~0.0005 (0.06%) gap is exactly the p_inf correction
(p_inf = 0.05243 m²/s², itself ~0.05% of q), not a data or pipeline
inconsistency. The properly-normalized figure (0.8892) is the one carried
in the currently published validation record and is the more rigorous
definition of Cp.
