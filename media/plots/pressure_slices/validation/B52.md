# B-52 surface-pressure field: triple-check before it stays on camera

> **CORRECTED 2026-07-26.** This check missed a reporting defect that was
> inside its own scope. The original text below is kept intact; the section
> **"Correction: the reported range was the colour clip"** at the end of this
> document states what was wrong, what the corrected numbers are, and what
> still stands. Read that section before quoting any range from this page.

Scope: the painted B-52 surface pressure served to the GUI
(`mission-output/geometry-study/b52_field.json`), the wireframe display mesh
behind it, and the pressure the website slice was rendered from. Everything
below is read from the solved cases' own `foamToVTK` output.

Three things changed under this picture since the round-2 screenshot
(`demo-output/acts/round2/b52/03-cp-painted.png`, 2026-07-23, "15,660 faces",
Cd 0.0464, 193,815 cells, skew 5.06): the mesh was rebuilt under the
boundary-skewness gate, the geometry was rescaled from a generic 50 m length
to the published 48.5 m, and the GUI gained a display-only orphan-island
filter. Each is sized separately below.

## Verdict

**PASS.** Every physics invariant holds with margin and none is violated
anywhere on the body. The served JSON is the solver's own field to the last
digit. The orphan-island filter is proved to be display-only: the surviving
faces keep their own values and their own corner positions exactly.

The stated limit — not a failure — is that the nose stagnation peak reads
Cp 0.718 rather than approaching 1, because the faces carrying it are 0.41 m
across (0.9% of body length) and the peak is under-resolved. The pre-remesh
mesh reads 0.727 on the same measure, so this is the mesh scale, not the
remesh and not the rescale.

## 1. Physics invariants (all pass)

- **p_inf.** 0.0098 m2/s2, median cell pressure beyond 3 body lengths radius
  (64,326 cells). 2e-6 of q — effectively zero.
- **Stagnation bound.** p_inf + U^2/2 = 5,000.01 m2/s2. Max wall p/rho =
  3,590.8, Cp 0.718 <= 1. **Zero faces above the bound**, anywhere on the
  body. Minimum Cp -1.048.
- **Why the peak is 0.718 and not ~1.** The faces carrying the top 0.5% of
  wall pressure are a median 0.41 m across, 0.9% of the 48.5 m length; a
  boundary value at that stand-off from a rounded radome cannot reach the
  stagnation value. The pre-remesh, pre-rescale case reads Cp 0.727 on the
  identical measure — the same shortfall, so it belongs to the mesh scale
  rather than to either change. This under-shoots, which is the conservative
  direction, and it is the same effect already declared for the NACA sections
  in `VALIDATION.md`.
- **Stagnation sits on forward-facing surfaces.** Of the top 0.5% (40 faces,
  threshold Cp 0.578), **100%** have a positive flow-axis normal component
  (mean 0.544, median 0.692). Location: 52.5% nose/cockpit, 30.0% wings and
  engine pods, 17.5% mid-fuselage, 0% tail fin. Nose and leading edges, as
  required.
- **Suction sits on acceleration zones.** The bottom 0.5% (38 faces,
  threshold Cp -0.614) is 76.3% on the wings and engine pods, 13.2%
  nose/cockpit shoulder, 10.5% mid-fuselage, 0% tail fin. **None** of them is
  forward-facing (frac_forward 0.0, mean flow-axis component -0.570): the
  suction set and the stagnation set are cleanly opposite in orientation,
  which is the discriminating check.
- **Far-field decay.** Mean |Cp| on upstream spherical shells: 0.00159 at
  1 L, 0.000226 at 2 L, 0.000086 at 3 L (6,949 / 1,689 / 3,620 cells). A
  factor 7 per body length, decaying to 1e-4 of q.

## 2. Old vs new: physics, rescale, rendering

### Remesh + rescale physics: small and measured

Both cases were reduced to area-weighted mean surface Cp in 41 bins of
normalised length z/L, so the 3% geometry rescale does not create a spurious

| | Value |
|---|---|
| RMS dCp over the 41 shared bins | **0.0052** |
| Max |dCp| | 0.0166, at z/L 0.67 |
| Area-weighted Cp percentiles, new | -0.431 / -0.161 / -0.080 / -0.006 / 0.330 (2/25/50/75/98) |

| Colour-window shift | **1.40% of the window span** |
| Cd | 0.0464 -> 0.0472, +1.72% |

### What the rescale alone is expected to do

The rescale is a real Reynolds shift, and a small one. Pressure coefficients
on a fixed shape are Reynolds-independent to first order; the friction
component follows roughly Re^(-1/7) in the turbulent flat-plate sense, so a
-3.0% Re shift predicts about **+0.44%** on skin friction. The measured total
Cd moved +1.72%, of which the Re effect accounts for a fraction — the
remainder is the remesh (skew 5.06 -> 3.91 changes exactly the boundary-face
interpolation the force integral reads). Both are small, both are in the
right direction, and neither is visible in the paint: the colour window moved
1.4% of its span.

### Rendering: no effect on this body

The painted B-52 is **not** decimated. Its single `body` patch triangulates
to 15,684 faces, below the 30,000-face threshold, so `_package_painted`
passes the values straight through and the 2026-07-25
decimation-correspondence fix (`b203327`) cannot have changed one pixel of
it. That is confirmed on the artifact rather than assumed: the round-2
screenshot caption reads "15,660 faces" — total equals shown then as well —
and the current field paints smoothly at neighbour-face correlation
**r = 0.952** over 23,447 shared-edge pairs. The pre-fix truncation paint
that speckled the decimated motorbike has no analogue here.

The 2nd/98th percentile colour clipping is likewise unchanged: the identical
index arithmetic is present in `_attach_field` at `7608e28` (2026-07-23),
before the round-2 screenshot.

Window (m2/s2): old case [-1846.96, 1095.15] -> new case [-1841.77, 1136.74].

## Correction: the reported range was the colour clip

*Added 2026-07-26. Nothing above is deleted. This section records one defect
this check did not catch, the corrected numbers, and which of the original
findings survive unchanged.*

### What was wrong

Section 1 of this document correctly measured the maximum wall pressure as
p/rho 3,590.8, Cp 0.718, and section 2 correctly recorded the served window
as `[-1841.77, 1136.74]` m2/s2, which is Cp -0.368 to **+0.227**. Both
numbers are right. The question never asked is why they differ by a factor of
three, and whether the second one was being published as the field's maximum.
It was.

`_attach_field` in `sdk/chief_engineer/field_render.py` clipped the painted
values to the 2nd and 98th percentile, which is a legitimate colour-mapping
device, and then wrote those two clipped numbers into the served JSON under
the keys `min` and `max`. A colorbar or a caption built from `field["max"]`
therefore stated Cp 0.227 as the peak surface pressure on a body whose actual
peak the solver put at Cp 0.718.

A second, smaller stage was also allowed to set the reported physics.
`_read_patch` prefers the per-point pressure array and averages it to face
centres, because that is what makes the painted surface smooth. On this body
the solver's own wall maximum is p/rho 3,590.78 and the nodal-averaged
version of the same patch reads 3,064.55, so the peak was already shaved
before the clip ran.

### Corrected numbers

Re-measured 2026-07-26 from the same solved case in the run cache
(`~/certonomous-runs/.solve-cache/b52-c193880-i300/300`, mesh
`~/certonomous-runs/.mesh-cache/b52/polyMesh`), single `body` patch, 7,533
wall cells, q = 5,000 m2/s2. The 7,533 wall-cell count and the wall maximum
of p/rho 3,590.8 both reproduce this document's own section 1 exactly, so
this is the same survey, read at a different stage.

| Stage | p/rho low | p/rho high | Cp low | Cp high |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Solver's own wall cells (the physics) | -5,240.99 | 3,590.78 | **-1.0482** | **+0.7182** |
| Nodal-averaged per face (display input) | -3,488.20 | 3,064.55 | -0.6976 | +0.6129 |
| **Reported before the fix** (2/98 clip of display faces) | -1,841.77 | 1,136.74 | **-0.3684** | **+0.2273** |
| **Reported after the fix** (solver wall cells) | -5,240.99 | 3,590.78 | **-1.0482** | **+0.7182** |
| Colour window after the fix (unchanged, now labelled as such) | -1,841.8 | 1,136.7 | -0.3684 | +0.2273 |

The reported high moved from Cp 0.2273 to Cp 0.7182, a factor of 3.2. The
reported low moved from Cp -0.3684 to Cp -1.0482, a factor of 2.8. The
corrected values agree with section 1's independently measured wall maximum
Cp 0.718 and minimum Cp -1.048 to the digits printed there.

**Plainly stated: the gap between the raw wall Cp 0.718 and the displayed and
published Cp 0.227 was this reporting defect, not mesh resolution alone.**
This is the correction that matters most on this page, because section 1
offered a mesh-resolution explanation and that explanation was being read as
covering the whole shortfall. It does not. The two shortfalls are separate
and both are real:

- **Cp 1.0 down to Cp 0.718 is mesh resolution.** That finding stands
  unchanged. The faces carrying the top 0.5% of wall pressure are a median
  0.41 m across, 0.9% of the 48.5 m length, and the pre-remesh case reads
  0.727 on the identical measure. This is a genuine, conservative
  under-resolution of the radome stagnation point.
- **Cp 0.718 down to Cp 0.227 was the reporting defect**, and it is now
  fixed. No mesh change was involved: the solver had the 0.718 on file the
  whole time and the published range simply did not report it.

Cp here is `(p - p_inf) / q` with the p_inf of 0.0098 m2/s2 that section 1
measured. On this body that reference is 2e-6 of q, so every figure in the
table is unchanged to four decimals whether p_inf is subtracted or not.

### The bound flag: this body passes

Cp cannot exceed 1 at a stagnation point in incompressible flow, and a
reported physical range is now checked against that bound and carries the
result in `field["cp"]` of the served JSON. The B-52 passes cleanly:

| Key | B-52 value |
|---|---|
| `q_kinematic`, `p_inf` | 5,000, 0.0098 |
| `faces` | 7,533 |
| `min`, `max` | -1.0482, +0.7182 |
| **`within_stagnation_bound`** | **true** |
| `over_bound.count` | **0** |
| `over_bound.max_cp_within_bound` | +0.7182 |
| `suction_outliers.count` | 0 below Cp -2 |
| `caveat` | absent, because there is nothing to disclose |

This confirms section 1's "zero faces above the bound" result at the
reporting layer rather than only in a one-off survey, and it is the clean
counterpart to the motorbike, which fails the same check on a single
sliver-cell face. Note that Cp -1.0482 is **not** a violation: Cp has no hard
lower bound, and the flag deliberately does not test for one.

### The fix

`sdk/chief_engineer/field_render.py` now reports two clearly distinct ranges:

- `min`/`max`: the physical range, read from the solver's own `CellData` wall
  values before any interpolation, clustering or clipping.
- `color_min`/`color_max` (with `display_min`/`display_max` kept as aliases
  for the existing GUI legend): the 2nd/98th percentile colour window, a
  display device only.
- `cp`: the stagnation-bound disclosure described above, present whenever the
  case's q is known.

The GUI legend in `sdk/chief_engineer/control_room.html` now tags the colour
bar as the colour range, prints the physical range beside it, and shows the
bound warning when one is present. `sdk/tests/test_field_render.py` pins the
invariant.

### What still stands, unchanged

- Every physics invariant in section 1, including zero faces above the
  stagnation bound. Those were read from the solver's own wall values and are
  unaffected.
- The mesh-resolution explanation for the 0.718 peak, as set out above.
- Section 2's remesh-and-rescale attribution: RMS dCp 0.0052, Cd 0.0464 to
  0.0472.
- Section 2's "rendering: no effect on this body" finding. The B-52 is
  genuinely not decimated (15,684 faces, below the 30,000 threshold), so the
  clustering half of the defect never touched it. Only the nodal-averaging
  and clipping halves did, and those apply to decimated and non-decimated
  bodies alike, which is why this body was affected despite passing the
  decimation check.
- All of section 3, the orphan-island filter and served-JSON integrity work.
  The served per-face values were, and are, the solver's own. The defect was
  in the two summary numbers alongside them.
