\textbf{The question, and the trap inside it}

\vspace{1pt}
{\footnotesize A drag reduction is only worth what it costs in lift. The failure
mode is a wing flown at a lower angle of attack. It sheds lift and reports the
drag saving as though the lift is still there. Every intermediate design is
solved at matched lift, with the lift printed beside every drag.
\textbf{Only rows at the same lift compare on drag}, and Table~1 is the only
table here a drag attribution comes from.}
