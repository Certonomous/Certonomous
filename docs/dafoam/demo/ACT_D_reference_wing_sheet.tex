{\fontsize{8.6}{10}\selectfont The $28.3\,\%$ on the reference wing, taken apart

{\color{rulegrey}\rule{\textwidth}{0.9pt}}\\[1pt]
{\footnotesize \textbf{This sheet reports a demonstration, not a certified
verdict.} A DAFoam verdict in this lab is two rows, one against the shipped
toolchain and one against whatever was patched; only the shipped row exists
here, so the act as a whole carries no grade. The fixed verdict words used
below --- \textsc{pass}, \textsc{not a result} --- belong to the individual
gates, which were written down before the run and did earn them. Nothing on
this page grades the optimisation.}

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.545\textwidth}
\textbf{The question, and the trap inside it}

\vspace{1pt}
{\footnotesize A drag reduction is only worth what it costs in lift. The
failure mode we were asked to rule out is the one where a wing is quietly flown
at a lower angle of attack, sheds most of its lift, and reports the drag saving
as though the lift were still there. So we re-solved the wing at every
intermediate design and printed the lift beside every drag. \textbf{Only rows
at the same lift may be compared}, and Table~1 is the only table here a drag
attribution may be quoted from.}

\vspace{3pt}

Every row re-solved; drag never quoted without its lift.

\vspace{1.5pt}
{\scriptsize \textbf{Total reduction $\mathbf{28.2768\,\%}$}, baseline to final,
both endpoints at $C_L=0.500$. Shares are
$(C_D^{\mathrm{stage}}-C_D^{\mathrm{base}})/(C_D^{\mathrm{final}}-C_D^{\mathrm{base}})$;
both were predicted before the run inside admissible bands --- twist $-5$ to
$+8\,\%$, section shape $92$ to $105\,\%$ --- and \textbf{both measured shares
landed inside them}. The angle-of-attack share is zero by construction, not by
measurement: lift is an equality constraint at $C_L=0.5$, so at matched lift the
incidence is slaved to the geometry.}

\vspace{3pt}
{\footnotesize\colorbox{band}{\begin{minipage}[t]{0.978\textwidth}
\textbf{The finding that goes against our own story.} \textbf{Twist alone, at
matched lift, makes the drag $0.26\,\%$ \emph{worse}} --- $0.02969750$ against
$0.02962051$ --- because recovering the lift it sheds costs more incidence
($4.909^\circ$ against $4.326^\circ$) than the spanload change saves.
\textbf{Essentially all of the $28.3\,\%$ is section shape.} Twist pays only in
combination with it. That is not the tidier answer, and it is the measured one.
\end{minipage}}}

{\footnotesize\textbf{Read this before you use the numbers}\\[1pt]
$\bullet$ \textbf{One registered check did not run, and the claim it was written
to falsify is therefore not confirmed by it.} Row B2 was to re-trim the
twisted, reshaped wing back to $C_L=0.5$ from an independent start and land on
the final row within $0.5\,\%$ --- gate G5, the independent falsifier for the
statement that the angle-of-attack share is zero. Its flow solve diverged after
three attempts and produced no value. \textbf{B2 is \textsc{not a result} and
G5 is ungraded.} The zero angle-of-attack share rests on the matched-lift
measurement (both endpoints at $C_L=0.500$ to better than $10^{-3}$, gate G6)
and on the problem's own lift equality constraint, and on nothing else.
\textbf{No test fired to confirm it.}\\
$\bullet$ \textbf{$38\,304$-cell mesh, chosen for speed --- grid independence
not assessed; result relative to this mesh.} Grid independence was not assessed
in this act.\\
$\bullet$ \textbf{The optimisation was not run to convergence and we do not
claim it was.} It completed $47$ major iterations; over the last fifteen the
objective moved inside a $2.2\,\%$ band and $12$ of the $14$ steps still took
drag down, so it was still descending when it stopped. \textbf{Why it stopped,
established from its own log:} the log records no termination --- no exit line,
no convergence statement --- and its iteration limit was set to $100$, so it
was \emph{not} an iteration cap; at the last iteration its two first-order
measures read $1.44\times10^{-5}$ and $9.0\times10^{-5}$ against a $10^{-5}$
target. \textbf{It was ended by a sixty-minute wall clock.}\\
$\bullet$ \textbf{The gradient was graded at a single finite-difference step,
$h=10^{-3}$, and the sweep proving that step sits on a plateau was run on a
different, smaller case} --- a two-dimensional section on $4\,032$ cells, where
the recommended window measured $10^{-3}$ to $10^{-2}$. \textbf{No step sweep
was run on this wing.}\\
$\bullet$ \textbf{Toolchain, named by hash, because a version string is not an
identity.} Both the optimisation and this decomposition ran the shipped image
\texttt{dafoam/opt-packages:latest}, digest
\texttt{sha256:9d45679d55fd47f5ca7afd99}\allowbreak\texttt{cabb86c7c2729cf2acf34c438eb33af5290f07fc},
with stock IDWarp \texttt{libidwarp.so} md5
\texttt{f0fcb488e0e98156575cd19548e91663}. \textbf{No patched library was
loaded}: the launcher carries no bind-mount and no override, and the patched
solver's own banner is absent from the log --- this lab's standing proof that a
run was stock.\\
$\bullet$ Nothing here is compared against wind-tunnel or flight data. The
gradient is checked against finite differences of the same solver: that is
self-consistency, not validation.}
