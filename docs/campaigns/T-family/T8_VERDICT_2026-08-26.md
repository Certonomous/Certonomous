## 1. Why the label matters, and why `BLOCKED` is the wrong one

`BLOCKED` is for a **blocker** — a rung that could not run, or could not be
graded, for want of something external. **T8 ran.** It fired three levels,
produced three complete measurement trees, and spent **224.667 core-minutes**.
**Three measurements that fail the gate is a different and far more informative
state than an inability to measure**, and the vocabulary distinguishes them
(`CLAUDE.md` rule 1; `VERIFICATION_CHARTER.md` §2).

Recording `BLOCKED` here would discard the rung's actual product — **a
three-level, grid-spanning demonstration that the registered formulation does not
converge** — and file it as an absence.

**The following records still read `BLOCKED` and are owed a correction by their

## 3. GROUND 2 — the axis-extrapolation precondition is false, and this ground needs no convergence at all

§12 S3 of the frozen pre-registration registers the axis extrapolation
`(9f₁ − f₂)/8`, whose precondition is **`r₂ = 3·r₁`**. Measured on the built
level-`c` mesh from the cell centres OpenFOAM itself wrote: **`r₂/r₁ =
2.333313`**, against the exact wedge-centroid value **`7/3 = 2.333333`** — **not
3.** `resolve_planes` refuses. **No row can be graded at all.**

**Cite both grounds.** They share no instrument: one reads solver convergence
from written fields, the other reads mesh geometry from written cell centres.
**A verdict resting on two independent grounds is worth far more than one resting
on a single reading**, and either alone is sufficient here.

## 4. THE TRANSFERABLE LESSON, and the supervisor names it as the rung's main product

> ### **READ CENTROIDS FROM DISK. NEVER REGISTER A RATIO TAKEN FROM THE NOMINAL MESH SPEC.**

A ratio asserted from geometry-on-paper is **not a measured ratio**. T8's
registration asserted `3` where the wedge volume centroids give `7/3`, and it did
so **before anything could check** — the fixture that was supposed to control it
placed cell centres at `(j+½)·dr`, making `r₂ = 3r₁` **true by construction**, so
the fixture and the instrument agreed **because they shared one wrong
assumption**. 69 selftest checks passed and not one could have caught it.

**The magnitude is not the point and must not be used to minimise it.** The
flat-sided correction moves `w₁` by `4.73e-06`, which on a 20 K field difference
is `9.5e-05 K` — negligible against ±0.05 bands. **The defect is not numerical.
It is that a geometric fact about a mesh was ASSERTED rather than MEASURED, and
the control shared the assertion.** The successor's instrument reads `r₁` and
`r₂` from the `Cx`/`Cy` OpenFOAM wrote, asserts only the **structural** fact
`r₂ > r₁ > 0`, and **never asserts a ratio**
(`T8_REREGISTRATION_DRAFT_2026-08-25.md` §2).

> ### **THE REGISTERED STEADY FORMULATION REACHES A CONVERGED STATE AT NO RESOLUTION, IN THREE DIFFERENT WAYS.**
>
> **One level's failure is a case. Three levels failing three different ways is a

The mesh-quality explanation is **dead**; the setup-difference explanation is
