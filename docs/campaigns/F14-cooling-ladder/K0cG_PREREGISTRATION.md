# K0cG. A third square-cavity mesh: giving the campaign's most-cited rung a discretisation bound

Campaign F14, gate K0c. Written 2026-08-19, **before any case was solved**.
Run tree `verification/runs/F14-cooling-ladder/K0cG_runs/`.

---

## 1. Why

**D428 gave the tall cavity's hi-Ra rung an error attribution and, in doing so,
showed the square cavity has none.** `K0cX` had three levels; `K0cS` has two, so
its deviations — `kOmegaSST` **+13.4 %**, `kEpsilon` **+17.1 / +20.5 %** on
hot-wall Nusselt — **cannot be separated into model error and discretisation
error at all.**

`K0cS` is the rung this campaign cites most: `K0cQ`, `K0cR` and `K0cP` all
measure against its recorded baselines. **An unbounded discretisation error sits
underneath every one of those comparisons.**

**This rung adds the third level and nothing else.**

---

## 2. Design — 2 cases, one mesh level, no new physics

The `K0cS` builder is parameterised at a refinement ratio of **1.6**:
120x120 with first cell 2.500e-04 m, then 192x192 with 1.5625e-04 m. **The third
level continues the same ladder**: **307x307**, first cell **9.7656e-05 m**,
which is 192 x 1.6 rounded to integer and 1.5625e-04 / 1.6 exactly.

| Case | Model | Mesh | cells | first cell | `endTime` |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `S_SST_x` | `kOmegaSST` | 307x307 | 94 249 | 9.7656e-05 | 40 000 |
| `S_KE_x` | `kEpsilon` | 307x307 | 94 249 | 9.7656e-05 | 40 000 |

**`endTime` is held at the FINE mesh's 40 000** so the three levels are compared
at the same iteration count, as `K0cS` compared its two. Convergence is measured
rather than assumed and is reported per case.

**`LaunderSharmaKE` is not run.** `K0cS` **REFUSED** it for missing the
convergence criterion, so it has no graded two-level baseline for a third level
to extend. Adding it would be a new rung, not a third mesh.

### 2.1 The builder is a copy, and the copy is auditable

`K0cG_runs/build_cases.py` is `K0cS_runs/build_cases.py` with **exactly three
constants changed** — `NX_FINE`, `NY_FINE` and `REFINE` — and the case dictionary
reduced to the two cases above. **The diff is committed with this rung.** Every
other dictionary, field, scheme, relaxation factor and boundary condition is
generated by the same code that generated the meshes it extends.

**The K0cS tree is not written to.** The new cases are built into `K0cG_runs/`.

## 3. Registered predictions

D428 measured the tall cavity's behaviour. **The square cavity is a different
geometry and this rung is registered without assuming it repeats.**

| Quantity | Registered prediction | What would refute it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `kOmegaSST` `Nu_hot` | mesh spread across three levels **small** relative to the 13.4 % deviation, as on the tall cavity where it was 68x | a spread comparable to the deviation, which would mean the square cavity's `kOmegaSST` error is substantially numerical |
| `kEpsilon` `Nu_hot` | **worse under refinement**, continuing the coarse -> fine trend of +17.15 -> +20.55 % | the deviation falling at the third level, which would make the two-level trend a coincidence |
| Observed order | **reportable for at most a minority of quantities**, given 1 of 6 on the tall cavity | clean monotone convergence throughout, which would say the tall cavity's behaviour is geometry-specific |

**The `kEpsilon` prediction is the sharp one.** Its two-level trend already
points the wrong way and D428 found the same model diverging on the tall cavity.
**A third point that continues it makes "refinement makes `kEpsilon` worse" a
two-geometry statement rather than a one-geometry one.**

---

## 4. What this rung cannot do

- **It grades nothing and no `K0cS` verdict can move.** `K0cS` graded its fine
  mesh under its own registered rule. A third level changes the **attribution**
  of the deviation, not the deviation.
- **It cannot rescue a straddled row**, because it adds a level rather than
  changing a band. If a row straddles, this rung reports that it straddles, as
  D428 did for `LaunderSharmaKE` on the tall cavity.
- **Two cases are not six.** The controls, `LaunderSharmaKE`, and the lo-Ra
  question are all untouched.

## 5. Cost

`S_SST_f` 2170.42 s and `S_KE_f` 3055.73 s at 36 864 cells. The third level is
**94 249 / 36 864 = 2.557x** the cells at the same iteration count, and work per
iteration is close to linear in cell count for these solvers.

**Estimate: 2.557 x (2170.42 + 3055.73) = 13 363 s = 222.7 core-minutes =
3.71 core-hours = $0.190.** Wall clock about 2.2 hours with both cases
concurrent, set by `S_KE_x`.

**This estimate carries no contingency factor and is expected to be an
over-estimate**, because both cases run concurrently on an otherwise idle
machine, which is the condition under which `K0cQ` and `K0cP` both came in under
their estimates.

---

## 6. Falsifier

**Exhibit a mesh spread on `kOmegaSST` `Nu_hot` across the three levels that is
comparable to its 13.4 % deviation.** That would make the square cavity's most
cited number substantially numerical and would put every comparison built on it
— `K0cQ`, `K0cR`, `K0cP` — back in question.
