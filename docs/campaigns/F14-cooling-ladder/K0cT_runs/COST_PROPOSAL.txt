THE BASIS IS MEASURED, NOT RECALLED
-----------------------------------
Two scratch pilots were run in the session scratchpad, OUTSIDE the case tree,
before this estimate was written.  Both are charged below.  Machine: AMD EPYC
9R14, single-core solves throughout, so core-minutes = wall clock.

  mesh          cells    iterations   solver ExecutionTime   s/iteration   s/(cell.iteration)
  40 x 120       4800       300            2.65 s            8.83e-03        1.84e-06
  64 x 192      12288       300            6.39 s            2.13e-02        1.73e-06

The two rates agree to 6 percent per cell, which is the check that the number
is a rate and not an artefact of one mesh.

CONTINGENCY ON THE RATE: 1.6x.  The pilots are 300 iterations into a developing
flow, where p_rgh converged in 3 PCG sweeps.  A developed buoyant field takes
more, and the rate is therefore an underestimate of the steady-state rate by an
amount this factor covers.  It is a stated multiplier, not a hidden one.

ITERATION COUNTS, and why they are what they are: the laminar rung of this same
gate needed 3000 iterations at Ra 1e3 and 12000 at Ra 1e6 on a 192x192 mesh,
and every one of its fine meshes had to be CONTINUED because an under-relaxed
SIMPLE outer loop moves the smooth modes at a rate falling off like 1/N^2.
This cavity is 28.7 aspect ratios tall, so its slowest mode is longer still.
20000 outer iterations are budgeted on every coarse mesh and 30000 on every
fine mesh, which is roughly 2.5x the laminar rung's worst case.

WHAT WOULD BLOW THIS ESTIMATE, NAMED IN ADVANCE
-----------------------------------------------
1. The case does not reach steady state.  Betts and Bokhari's own authors could
   not converge a steady solution on a related case and used transient
   averaging.  If a graded case here cannot meet the convergence criterion, the
   row is REPORTED AS REFUSED with its measured spread.  A transient leg is
   NOT run inside this authorization: at the same mesh a transient solve needing
   physical averaging over even 30 convective transits (H/V = 11.5 s each) is
   several hundred core-minutes, which is more than the whole ceiling.  It is
   proposed, not executed.
2. LaunderSharmaKE is a low-Reynolds-number k-epsilon with exponential damping
   functions and is stiffer than kOmegaSST.  If it needs a second stage it is
   the most likely single consumer of the continuation reserve.
