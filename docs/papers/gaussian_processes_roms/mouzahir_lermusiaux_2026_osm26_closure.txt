• Complex nonlinear, multiscale fluid dynamics with unresolved sub-grid

• Regime variability (Reynolds number shifts, Transient forcing) and evolving
flow structures
Needs:
• Fast, online data-driven correction of LR dynamics embedded directly into the
solver
• Uncertainty-aware predictions of resolved fields and wake metrics
• Transferable across resolutions, domains, and flow conditions

Where:
• Uncertainty is minimal near the inlet BC due to high
correlation with Re and maximal near the cylinder due to
under resolved boundary layer
• Uncertainty grows over time due to distribution drift
