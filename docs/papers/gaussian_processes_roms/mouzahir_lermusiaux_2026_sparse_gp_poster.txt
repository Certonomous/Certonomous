●

Sparse variational GP closures, trained on snapshot pairs, inject mean (or stochastic) corrections directly into the FV solver and yield
stable OOD rollouts on Re(t) modulations the model never saw at training.
An analytical δR for AB2CN2 IMEX is derived: three pointwise-spectral terms + one NN surrogate (Ï). Empirical residuals match the
predicted closure to high accuracy on flow past a cylinder.
Together: the spatial closure mitigates grid-resolution bias; the temporal closure mitigates step-size bias. Combined, they target an endto-end fast, accurate, uncertainty-aware coarse solver.
