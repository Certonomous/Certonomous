16. Abstract

Current design philosophy
for hydrogen-fueled,
scramjet-powered
hypersonic
aircraft
results
in configurations
with strong couplings
between the engine plume
and vehicle
aerodynamics.
This report
describes
the experimental
verification
of the scramjet
exhaust simulation
devised by Grumman Research.
The scramjet
exhaust was reproduced
for the Mach 6 flight
condition
by the detonation
tube
simulator.
The exhaust flow pressure
profiles
, and to a large extent the heat
transfer
rate profiles,
were then duplicated
by cool gas mixtures
of Argon and
Freon 13Bl or Freon 12. Cool substitute
gas mixtures
such as these could be
used in conventional
wind tunnels.
The results
of these experiments
indicate
that a cool gas simulation
of the hot scramjet
exhaust is a viable
simulation
technique
except for phenomena which are dependent on the wall temperature
relative
to flow temperature.
