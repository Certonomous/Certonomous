PART D -- THE FORGE AND THE SELFTEST
===========================================================================

D1.  THE FORGE gains a development law
        T(j,i) = T_lin(i) + entry_amp * dT * ramp(y_j) * sin(pi Y_i)
     with y_j = (j+0.5)/N the PHYSICAL streamwise coordinate, so the law is
     IDENTICAL on all three levels and nothing is level-indexed.
     A SMOOTH decay was tried and REJECTED for a real reason, not convenience:
     under any smooth decay the "smallest j" rule lands the station EXACTLY at
     the floor crossing, the three-level clause then turns on a mapping
     residual of up to half a coarse cell, and the limb would measure the
     forge's steepness rather than the rule.  With a ramp to exactly zero the
     crossing is unambiguous on every level.
     THAT FRAGILITY IS NOT A FORGE ARTEFACT AND IS NOT HIDDEN: on a real solve
     the station WILL sit at the floor crossing, and how much margin c and m
     then have at the same y/b is not something this comparator can promise.
     It PRINTS each level's W1_T and mapping residual for exactly that reason.
D2.  `bump_about_b` IS ADDED BECAUSE THE STATION MOVED.  analyse_t16.py's forge
     indexes its witness and mass perturbations off `build_t16.STATION_B` --
     T16's registered station at 48b.  T16c grades where section 4 selects, so
     a perturbation at 48b lands 40b from the graded row and the W1 and C_MASS
     limbs would go green while testing nothing.  MEASURED as two dead limbs,
     not reasoned about.  This is the first thing the repair breaks in the
     inherited harness and is recorded as such.
D3.  `_carried_over_identity()` is new.  Frozen section 6 CLAIMS the carried
     content is byte-identical; this MEASURES it by AST source extraction
