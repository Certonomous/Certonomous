CONTROL PREDICTIONS -- F14 rung K0c, laminar differentially heated cavity
Registered before any control result was read.  Every number below is derived

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
C1  Ra1e5_m128 twin with g = 0.   KIND: RECOGNITION (gross), and it also
    demonstrates REACHABILITY of the comparator's FAIL branch.

    With g = 0 there is no buoyancy source in the momentum equation, U stays
    identically zero, and the cavity solves pure 1-D conduction between two
    isothermal walls with two adiabatic walls.  For that solution
    dT/dx = -dT/L everywhere and Nu(y) = 1 exactly at every y.

    PREDICTED:  Nu_avg = 1.000, Nu_max = 1.000, Nu_min = 1.000,
                u1max = u2max = 0.000, max|U| = 0 exactly.
    PREDICTED GATE VERDICT: FAIL.  REL(Nu_avg) = 100*|1.000-4.519|/4.519
                = 77.87 percent against a 1.0 percent band.
    IN-LOG WITNESS OF THE PLANT: `Solving for Ux/Uy` initial residuals collapse
                to zero, and areaNormalIntegrate(hotWall) printed by the running
                solver equals the conduction value.  The wall temperatures
                printed by hotT/coldT must be UNCHANGED from the graded case,
                which is what distinguishes this plant from C2's.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
C2  Ra1e5_m128 twin with dT raised by exactly 1.10, i.e. Ra = 1.10e5.
    KIND: RECOGNITION AT THE GATE'S OWN SCALE.  This is the control that
    answers "can this gate fail?" for the row that actually grades the rung.

    The gate bands Nu_avg at 1.0 percent.  The question is whether that band can
    resolve a realistic error in the driving parameter.  The exponent comes from
    the gate's OWN reference table (Section 1.3), not from a correlation:
        ln(4.519/2.243)/ln(10) = 0.3040   (Ra 1e4 -> 1e5)
        ln(8.800/4.519)/ln(10) = 0.2895   (Ra 1e5 -> 1e6)
    so locally Nu ~ Ra^n with n in [0.289, 0.304], and
        dNu/Nu = n * ln(1.10) = n * 0.09531  =  2.76 to 2.90 percent.

    PREDICTED:  Nu_avg on the C2 case lands 2.6 to 3.0 percent ABOVE the
                Ra = 1e5 graded value, i.e. Nu_avg = 4.63 to 4.66.
    PREDICTED GATE VERDICT: FAIL against the Ra = 1e5 reference 4.519, with
                REL between 2.6 and 3.0 percent versus a 1.0 percent band.
    IF IT PASSED, the 1.0 percent band would be unable to see a 10 percent
    error in the Rayleigh number and the rung's pass would mean nothing.
    IN-LOG WITNESS OF THE PLANT: hotT/coldT are printed by the RUNNING solver
    every 50 iterations; their difference must equal 1.196978 K, exactly 1.10x
    the 1.088162 K of the graded case.  A plant that existed only in 0.orig/T
    would not move these lines.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
C3  Ra1e5_m64 twin with a planted uniform volumetric source of 5.000e-03 W.
    KIND: REACHABILITY of the energy-balance row, plus a stated limit on what
    that row can ever prove.

    K0b's C3 predicted a large heat imbalance on an unconverged sealed cavity
    and measured 0.013 percent, because on a closed impermeable steady case the
    discrete T equation conserves at every iteration whether or not the field
    has converged: the boundary heat balance there is very nearly an IDENTITY
    and cannot gate anything (W-2).  That finding applies unchanged to this
    gate's energy-balance row, which is stated in the report rather than hidden.
    C3 therefore does not try to make the row fail on convergence error.  It
    plants a real conservation defect and checks the row sees it.

    PREDICTED:  sum of wall fluxes recovers -5.000e-03 W to within 0.1 percent
                (the source leaves the domain through the walls at steady
                state, so the boundary integral must equal minus the source).
    PREDICTED GATE VERDICT on the energy-balance row: FAIL, with
                |Nu_hot - Nu_cold|/Nu_avg of order tens of percent, against a
                0.5 percent band.
    IN-LOG WITNESS OF THE PLANT: buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam echoes fvOptions
    at construction; the log must contain the source name `heatPlant` and its
    cell-set selection.  A source present in constant/fvOptions but not
    constructed by the solver would not appear there.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
C4  Comparator mutation.  NO COMPUTE.  KIND: REACHABILITY of every band in the
    gate table, individually.

    C1 to C3 prove that some rows can fail.  They do not prove that the other
    rows are not hard-wired to pass.  C4 perturbs each solved value in turn by a
    factor just outside its own band and asserts that exactly that row flips to
    FAIL and no other row moves.

    PREDICTED: every one of the gate's rows flips to FAIL under its own
    perturbation and under no other row's.  Any row that stays PASS under a
    perturbation larger than its band is a row that grades nothing.
