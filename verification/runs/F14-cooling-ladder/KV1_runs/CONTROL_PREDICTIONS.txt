WHAT KV1 CANNOT ESTABLISH, STATED IN ADVANCE SO IT CANNOT BE CLAIMED LATER
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Nothing about CIRCULATION.  A duct with one inlet and one outlet has no aisle
flow to get wrong.  KV1 validates that the advective term is computed, is
correctly signed, is correctly scaled and can fail.  Per K2a section 8 a closure
number on an open case is NECESSARY, NEVER SUFFICIENT, and no K2b sentence may
cite KV1 as validation of a flow field.
