RULING (cfd-supervisor, 2026-09-10): SUBOFF R1 uses ONE PINNED forceCoeffs Aref at
every grid level.  A_ref is a definitional reference area in Cd = F/(0.5 rho U^2 A_ref),
not a mesh property; a per-level Aref would divide out how well each mesh represents the
body's wetted area -- which IS part of the discretisation error -- and would inject a
monotone 0.171% normalisation drift into the Roache triple.

  Aref PINNED (analytic / 72)           = 0.083170336281 m^2
  written to system/controlDict as        0.08317033628
Provenance of the analytic area: Groves/Huang/Chang 1989 (DTRC/SHD-1298-01) TABLE 1,
report p.4 / PDF p.11, title-page verified; area RE-DERIVED by independent numerical
quadrature of the surface of revolution by cases/navier_class/SUBOFF/derive_wetted_area.py
(NOT a facet sum, NOT hull_sector_area()).  The inherited four-figure 5.988 m^2 is
