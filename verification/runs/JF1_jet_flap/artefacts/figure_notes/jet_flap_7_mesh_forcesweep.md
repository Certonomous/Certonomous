# The grid the lift and pressures are computed on

A different grid from the flow-field page and a different shape of grid: an O-shape that closes around the wing rather than trailing behind it. All five calculations ran on this one grid, 39,984 cells, one cell deep, 10 mm span. Four had the slot open and blowing at different strengths, so the differences among those four come from the blowing alone; the fifth is a reference case with the slot closed. It is drawn cell by cell from the stored grid of the solved cases, with no surface tessellation anywhere.

This grid is not the grid on the flow-field page. The two are shown on separate pages and never on shared axes. 12 cells span the 5.0 mm slot here as well, each 0.417 mm tall. The largest cell distortion is 57.5 degrees, the largest skew 2.30, and no cell has zero or negative volume.

Near-wall spacing rises with blowing, from largest 0.191 with the slot closed to largest 0.398 at the strongest jet. Every wall cell of every calculation is below the wall-resolved limit. The page is captioned as the grid all five calculations ran on, so this is the worst of the five and not the one case the figure renders. None of the five reached its convergence target; the lift they report had stopped moving.
