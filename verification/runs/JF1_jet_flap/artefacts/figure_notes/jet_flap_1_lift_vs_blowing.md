# Lift against blowing

None of these calculations reached the convergence target fixed before they ran, which was all five solution channels below 1e-06. The turbulence-energy channel is the slowest everywhere and worsens with blowing, from 3.5e-06 with no jet to 1.5e-04 at the strongest jet, a factor of 43; strong blowing makes this flow genuinely hard to converge, which is a property of the physics rather than of any one calculation.

Over the final 4,000 iterations the lift still moves by up to 3.4×10⁻⁵ at the worst row. That is a settling indicator and not convergence, and the bars drawn for it are smaller than the symbols.

Mesh sensitivity has not been quantified for these points: no refinement study was run, so no grid uncertainty is claimed. Treat every number here as indicative. It is not a validated result and it has not been compared against experiment.
