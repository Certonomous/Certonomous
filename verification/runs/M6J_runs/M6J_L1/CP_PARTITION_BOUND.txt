THE INTEGRAL PERTURBATION SATURATES.  It rises from 2.7e-08 at the first step
after the seam to a plateau of roughly 2e-06 absolute (~1e-04 relative on
Cd ~ 0.0187) by about 200 iterations, and does NOT grow thereafter.  No runaway.

WHAT IS AND IS NOT MEASURED
  MEASURED: the LOCAL Cp bound at ONE time, t=4200, 400 iterations after the seam.
  MEASURED: the INTEGRAL perturbation saturates within ~200 iterations.
  NOT MEASURED: whether the LOCAL Cp perturbation itself saturates.  It was taken
    at a single time because a second point needs a second control run, and the
    graded run's t=4000 was destroyed by purgeWrite before it could be rescued.
    The integral trend gives NO EVIDENCE OF DIVERGENCE, and that is the strongest
    statement the evidence supports.  It is not a proof that Cp at t=8000 is
    perturbed by no more than 3.83e-03.
