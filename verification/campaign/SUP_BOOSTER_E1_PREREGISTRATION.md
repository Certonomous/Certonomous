## 1. Formulation (one chosen, frozen here)

A **sharp cone at zero incidence in steady supersonic flow**. The flow is **conical**
(self-similar along rays from the apex) and its exact inviscid solution is the
Taylor-Maccoll ODE — distinct from the 2-D wedge (oblique-shock) solution, which is why the
mesh is a **true axisymmetric wedge**, never a planar slab.

- **Freestream Mach** M∞ = **2.0**. **Cone half-angle** θ_c = **15.0°**. γ = 1.4 (air).
- **Strongly-attached-shock regime, no detachment.** The maximum (detachment) cone
  half-angle at M∞ = 2.0 is ≈ 40.5°; θ_c = 15° is far below it, so a real attached conical
  shock exists and the Taylor-Maccoll shooting converges cleanly. The regenerated shock
  angle β = 33.915° lies above the Mach angle (μ = arcsin(1/2) = 30°) and well below
  detachment — an unambiguous, strongly-attached conical shock. This is the canonical
  textbook TM verification case (Anderson, *Modern Compressible Flow* Ch. 10; NACA 1135
  conical-flow tables), maximising independent cross-checkability even though the EXACT tier
  needs no external number.
- **Solver: `rhoCentralFoam`** (openfoam2606), Kurganov flux, vanLeer reconstruction, LTS
  (`localEuler`) driven to a steady pseudo-transient state — the lab's supersonic workhorse
  and the proven F3/F4/DMR stack. **Inviscid (Euler): mu = 0, laminar, slip cone wall** —
  this is the correct match to the Taylor-Maccoll reference, which is an **inviscid** ODE.
- **Geometry (metres):** apex at origin, axis = +x; cone length L = 1.0; base radius
  L·tan(15°) = 0.2679; upstream inlet buffer x_min = −0.5; far-field radius R_top = 1.2.
- **Boundary conditions:** inlet (left) and freestream (top) = supersonic fixedValue
  freestream (U = (694.40, 0, 0) m/s from M∞·a∞, a∞ = √(1.4·287.06·300) = 347.20;
  T = 300 K; p = 101325 Pa); outlet (right) = supersonic outflow (zeroGradient U, p, T);
  cone = **slip** wall (U slip, p/T zeroGradient — inviscid); axis = `empty`; front/back =
  `wedge` (2.5° half-angle, total 5°, 1 cell thick).
- **Farfield placement (Spalart–Rumsey cross-cut, EXTERNAL case).** For steady *supersonic*
  external flow the classical subsonic far-field pressure-propagation sensitivity does not
  apply: disturbances cannot propagate upstream of the attached shock. The binding
  requirement is that the **attached conical shock crosses a supersonic OUTFLOW boundary**,
  not a fixed-value inflow. R_top = 1.2 > L·tan(β) = 0.673 guarantees the shock exits the
  **outlet** (right), never the top; the top boundary sits entirely in undisturbed
  freestream above the shock, so a fixedValue freestream BC there is exact. Verified at
  pre-flight.
- **Blockage correction: N/A** — unbounded external cone (no tunnel walls). Stated and
  dismissed, per the tasking.

## 3. Gates, thresholds, labels

Both gates are decided **only** through the Roache triple over the three grids (§4); a
non-`CONVERGING` triple, or any level not iteratively plateaued, is **`NOT A RESULT`**
whatever the number (rule 5). GCI is reported at Celik F_s = 1.25.

- **Gate C1 — cone-surface Cp (PRIMARY).** Compare the fine-grid cone-surface Cp (trimmed
  plateau mean over the self-similar cone, apex-most 10% and outlet-most 5% dropped) to the
  regenerated reference. **PASS iff |Cp_fine − 0.202248| ≤ 0.010** (≈ ±5% of the reference),
  and the triple is `CONVERGING`; else **GATE FAIL** (or **NOT A RESULT** if the triple is
  not CONVERGING / not plateaued).
- **Gate C2 — conical shock angle β (SECONDARY).** Density-gradient shock locator over
  ≥ 3 self-similar x-stations, least-squares line required to pass within 5% L of the apex.
  **PASS iff |β_fine − 33.9147°| ≤ 1.0°** and the triple is `CONVERGING`; else GATE FAIL /
  NOT A RESULT. The ±1.0° band is instrument-expressible: the shock-region radial cell at
  the medium grid subtends ≈ 0.8° at a single station and ≈ 0.5° at the fine grid, both
  inside the band (multi-station fit tightens further).
- **Labels used:** `PASS`, `GATE FAIL`, `NOT A RESULT`, `PENDING`, `BLOCKED` (fixed
  vocabulary, rule 1). The rung's overall verdict is **PASS only if BOTH C1 and C2 PASS**;
  it is **NOT A RESULT** if either triple is non-CONVERGING/not-plateaued; otherwise the
  governing verdict is the worse of the two gates.

## 6. Planted-zero control (rule 3) — grader design

`verification/runs/navier_class/SUP_BOOSTER/grade_sup_booster.py` refuses to trust any clean read until the planted control fires:
before grading, it copies the finest-grid endTime `p` field, adds **PLANT = 1000.0 Pa** to
one cone owner-cell **inside the trimmed plateau**, reads the surface pressure back through
the *same* cone-Cp reader, and **requires** the reported plateau mean to rise by
PLANT / (plateau-face-count) to within 5%; otherwise it **REFUSES (exit 2)**. Exercised
live on the smoke case: planted 1000 Pa into cone cell 3611, reader delta = 10.6383 Pa =
expected 1000/94 = 10.6383 (exact) → control **PASSED**. A zero from a reader not shown able
to see a non-zero is not evidence.

## 7. Refuse-not-degrade & rule-4 completion (grader)

The grader **refuses (exit 2)** rather than degrades on any missing/malformed input or
mis-behaving control; **exit 70** only for an internal defect of the grader; **exit 0** only

DONE marker), **last time dir == endTime** (read from the case controlDict), the physics
fields **this** solver writes present at endTime, and each **newer than the case's own 0/T**
(age guard). **Field set:** the thermal-family list (`p_rgh alphat nut k omega phi`) does
**not** apply here — this is an inviscid compressible Euler case; the enforced set is
**`p U T rho`** (the fields rhoCentralFoam writes), stated explicitly so the departure from
the thermal list is disclosed, not silent.

## 8. Cross-cuts

- **Celik F_s = 1.25 three-level GCI:** §4; apparent order p and GCI_fine printed beside
  each gate; GCI never quoted for a non-monotone triple (rule 5).
- **Menter y+/wall treatment:** the EXACT rung is **inviscid (slip wall)** — there is no
  boundary layer, so **y+ is not a gate here** (N/A). Characterisation only: near-wall
  radial spacing ≈ 2.6 mm, giving a hypothetical *viscous* y+ ≈ 2000 at these conditions.
  The Menter y+/wall-treatment cross-cut therefore **binds the MEASURED-tier (viscous)
  successor**, which would require ~µm near-wall spacing and rides PENDING-data (§9).
- **Curvature LE-resolution check (scripts/check_le_surface_resolution.py):** this is a
  **rounded-LE-wing** admission mechanic (least-squares LE-circle fit, chordwise
  suction-peak spacing). SUP_BOOSTER is a **sharp cone (zero LE radius — a conical
  singularity at the apex, not a finite-radius leading edge)**, so its criteria (A) nose
  Δx/c and (B) LE-arc cell count are **geometrically inapplicable**; feeding a straight
  meridional line would trigger a false "(B) LE circle could not be fit" refusal that is a
  geometry fact, not a mesh defect. The checker's own planted controls were run and behave
  (coarse REFUSED, fine PASSED, exit 0), so the instrument is sound; it simply does not
  address this geometry. The **physics-based sizing that does matter for the cone** — apex
  and shock-band resolution — is applied via axial clustering toward the apex (shock origin)
  and radial clustering into the shock region; the shock is resolved at ≈ 14 mm radial cells
  at the medium grid (≈ 9 mm fine).
