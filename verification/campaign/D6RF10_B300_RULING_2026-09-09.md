# D6RF10 B-300 endTime-shortening — verdict-preservation / T25 ruling (verification supervisor, 2026-09-09)

**Question routed by the chief (gates the D6RF10 freeze).** D6RF10 (the D6RF9 confound-removal
successor) proposes shortening rungs R2/R3/R4 to `endTime 300` (R1 stays 2500). Sanaa endorsed
Option B-300; the chief set the engineering condition and delegated it to me: is B-300
**verdict-preserving / T25-clean** — i.e. does the shortened window make a PASS easier to reach, or
a FAIL harder to avoid?

**Gate under test.** G-CONV on `p_first_uncorrected` (the first uncorrected pressure initial residual
per outer block — the outer-loop / SIMPLE(C) convergence monitor); floor `CONV_BAR = 1.0e-5`; PASS iff
value `< 1.0e-5`, else GATE FAIL. The field DECREASES monotonically as the outer loop converges.

**The directional fact that bounds the risk.** Because `p_first_uncorrected` is monotone-decreasing,
an EARLIER stop reports a HIGHER (less-converged) residual than a later stop. Therefore B-300 reports a
**conservative (higher)** value on every rung and **structurally cannot manufacture a false PASS** — a
shorter window can never push the residual below the floor when the full window would not. The
anti-gaming direction ("make a PASS easier to reach") is safe on ALL rungs, unconditionally. The ONLY
live risk is the opposite direction: a rung whose residual would drop BELOW the floor only at a time
LATER than 300 would be spuriously GATE FAILed at 300 — "a FAIL harder to avoid" / a missed genuine
PASS.

**Evidence (dafoam, commit `f0750bcd`, extracted per the frozen grader's own parse).**
- **R1 (complete, endTime 2500), 26-point series:** `p_first_uncorrected` = 1.6305e-5 at Time 300 →
  1.6255e-5 at Time 2500; drift 300→2500 = 4.985e-8 (0.307% of value; 0.80% of the margin above the
  1e-5 floor), monotone decreasing. R1 is essentially PLATEAUED by Time 300, and its value is ABOVE the
  floor at both 300 and 2500 → GATE FAIL either way → verdict identical at 300 vs 2500.
- **R2 (partial to ~250, nNonOrth=12):** tracks R1 to 3–4 sig figs at common points, killed just before
  its own ~300 plateau, not diverging.
- **R3/R4 (SIMPLEC):** NO series exists — these are the rungs that crashed rc=59 on the decompose

**RULING.**

1. **R2 — B-300 SOUND (verdict-preserving / T25-clean).** Directly evidenced: R2 tracks R1's early
   plateau to 3–4 sig figs and is plateauing by ~300, not diverging; more correctors converge the outer
   loop faster-or-equal, so the deeper-corrector lever can only plateau EARLIER-or-equal, never later;
   and the monotone-decreasing direction makes B-300 conservative (cannot manufacture a PASS). R2's
   plateau (~1.6e-5) is above the floor → GATE FAIL at 300 = the endTime verdict. No missed PASS.

2. **R3/R4 (SIMPLEC) — B-300 NOT SOUND on the current evidence.** The plateau-by-300 for the SIMPLEC
   rungs is INFERRED from SIMPLE (R1/R2) data, and SIMPLEC is precisely the lever whose convergence
   timescale is UNMEASURED here. R1/R2 tracking tells us about the deeper-corrector lever on SIMPLE, NOT
   about SIMPLEC's velocity-pressure coupling, which can alter both the plateau value and the timescale.
   The chief's condition-3 risk — a SIMPLEC plateau LATER than 300 missing a genuine PASS ("a FAIL harder
   to avoid") — is REAL and un-refuted by SIMPLE data. B-300 for R3/R4 is therefore NOT verdict-preserving
   as currently evidenced.

3. **The remedy is the per-rung pre-flight standard being codified this session (§2bb).** B-300 for R3/R4
   becomes SOUND if and only if each SIMPLEC rung's OWN `p_first_uncorrected` trajectory is MEASURED in a
   per-rung pre-flight smoke (the §2bb solver-path smoke that ALSO clears the decompose-collision confound)
   and shown PLATEAUED by Time 300 (flat within a small tolerance; its plateau value then fixes the
   verdict). Absent that measured SIMPLEC plateau, R3/R4 keep a longer endTime (their measured plateau time,
   or 2500). This is exactly §2bb's deadline-sizing + solver-path validation, and it is where the pre-flight
   and the verdict-preservation test MEET (charter §2bb.4, final bullet).
