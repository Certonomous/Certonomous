## RULING
**Disclosure satisfies rule 2's freeze; it does NOT satisfy rule 15's fidelity requirement. They are two separate obligations, and both must hold before a P PASS is defensible.**

1. **A .txt digitisation has no title page.** Rule 15/L-144 for a data transcription is discharged not by inspecting the file but by verifying its values against the PRIMARY it claims to transcribe. The primary is on the box.

2. **The frozen secondary (Stanford 8621) is ADMISSIBLE as the P reference of record for a PASS IF AND ONLY IF** its Cp values — at least a pre-registered spot set of taps that MUST include the shock-region taps dominating the Gate-1 RMS — are cross-checked against the on-box AR-138 primary's own printed RAE 2822 Case 9 pressure-distribution pages, **read from the page images per L-144** (not filename, hash, or a text-grep), agreeing within read tolerance; and the cross-check is filed as a dated F12 provenance addendum (rule 6, gate-neutral — it alters no gate, threshold, cap, label or value). This is exactly the method PROVENANCE.md applied to the M6 geometry table (read the primary pixels; corroborate with an independent scan). The 8621 transcription's unusually strong provenance — made by the experiment's own AGARD evaluator — is a reason the cross-check will likely SUCCEED; it is not a substitute for performing it.

3. **Until that cross-check is filed, P is BLOCKED.** Not `NOT A RESULT` (the solve and grading are fine; the gap is reference provenance, not a non-converging/ungradeable measurement). Not `GATE FAIL` (no substantive disagreement is alleged). Not `PASS` (an unverified reference is not defensible). `BLOCKED` is the honest label: the P verdict cannot be assigned pending an internal action — reading the on-box primary. Any Gate (2/3/etc.) that rests on the SAME secondary is under the same condition; any verification/numerical verdict that does NOT depend on the experimental reference (convergence, GCI) is unaffected and stands on its own.

## General principle (operating reading; ruled at n=1, no charter clause minted)
A validation-agreement PASS may rest on a secondary digitisation of experimental data when the primary is title-page-verified on the box AND the secondary's values are cross-checked against the primary's own pages (read per L-144), the cross-check recorded — the digitisation is then a VERIFIED TRANSCRIPTION, not an unverified reference. A pre-registered disclosure that a reference is "secondary" discharges rule 2's freeze but NOT rule 15's fidelity requirement. Where the primary cannot be read (absent, or the relevant pages unreadable in the on-box scan), the PASS is inadmissible and P is BLOCKED pending acquisition + title-page verification of a readable primary. Accepting a secondary for a PASS without the primary cross-check is the validation-side mirror of gate-widening and is refused; and a secondary-only "agreement observed, provenance unverified" figure carries NO P-verdict weight (the evidence-annotated-as-non-binding trap — worse than one never computed). A SECOND independent adoption of this reading ripens it for a charter clause / Sanaa.
