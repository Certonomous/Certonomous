## Context (chief's reading, not her words)

- **The exemption class is exactly ONE, and it is narrow**: OpenFOAM
  genuinely cannot do the case (wrong solver family, a physically absent
  capability). A GATE FAIL or NOT A RESULT is acceptable as a TERMINAL state
  ONLY when unrecoverability-in-OpenFOAM is MEASURED and RECORDED — bug,
  solver selection, preconditioner, numerics/scheme, config, mesh, and
  model-form all explicitly ruled out. Anything short of that is a WAYPOINT,
  not a verdict the team may rest on.
- **The team's DUTY on any non-terminal fail is active and exhaustive**, in
  her words: fetch papers, look up solutions, look for bugs, re-read the
  manuals, read the OpenFOAM repositories/issues, and change what is wrong —
  "if it's the model, change the model; if it's the numerics, change the
  numerics" — until the case RUNS. This mandates INBOUND research as standing
  practice (reading papers, docs, upstream repos/issue trackers). It does NOT
  lift rule 7: SENDING anything out — posting to a forum, filing an upstream
  bug — remains PARKED and Sanaa's alone. Inbound research: mandated. Outbound
  contact: still parked.
- **Executed THROUGH the lab's law, never around it**: every fix is a
  SUCCESSOR registration or a §2an closure-ladder rung, pre-registered, with
  the gate/threshold/reference NEVER widened to manufacture a pass (her T25
  ruling stands). "Make the case run" means fix OUR side until the physics can
  speak; it never means adjusting the gate until the answer fits. The closure
  ladder (§2an: stock closures -> §0 numerical rule-out -> literature
  corrections -> GP closures) and the closure-correction library are the
  lab-wide resource for the "change the model" step.
- **CRM Rung 2 is now DIRECTED, not optional**: the designated family (cfd)
  must acquire/upload the committee grid LEVELS (L2, L3 alongside L1) so a
  Roache triple can form and the Rung-2 GCI ("CGI 2") can be computed. This
  moves CRM from "BLOCKED on a missing grid family" to "the team fetches the
  grid family." Grid acquisition is inbound (fetch/generate); it runs under
  the compute envelope and standing law.
- **Applies to EVERY team.** No fail stands unless proven an OpenFOAM
  capability gap; every other fail is worked to a run and its fix recorded as
  a lesson. This is the operative intensity behind the standing
  mandatory-completion order, not a new rule.
