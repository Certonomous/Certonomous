## Sanaa's words, verbatim

> [SANAA-DIRECT] Model-form GATE FAIL is a routing signal, not a terminus.
> When stock closures fail a gate, the case escalates into the closure ladder,
> in order, each rung gated against the same reference: (1) exhaust all stock
> RANS closures, register if none land; (2) rule out numerical cause per §0;
> (3) apply literature closure corrections — analytical terms,
> functional/nonlinear and algebraic-stress models, curvature/rotation and
> separation-specific corrections — each carried in the lab docs with its
> paper provenance and known-good flow class, applied and gated individually;
> (4) the lab's own research closures (GP-based) as the top rung. A
> model-form failure ships as a documented failure ONLY when the closure
> ladder is genuinely exhausted — and even then it ships with the full ladder
> recorded (which closures and corrections were tried, each deviation),
> because "all known closures fail this flow at these numbers" is itself a
> rare and valuable certified result. Process-class failures still halt as
> before; this ladder is for model-form only.
>
> Standing task for the lab: build the closure-correction library — ingest
> the closure-modeling literature (analytical, functional, algebraic-stress,
> data-informed, and the closure-challenge corrections) into the docs and
> lessons as installable, provenance-tagged closure options, each mapped to
> the flow class it addresses and its own validation case. This becomes a lab
> capability: given a model-form failure, propose and verify the correction
> the literature says should fix that flow class.
>
> Two practical notes. First — this makes the closure work you stood down
> strategically central again, but in the right frame: not a research project
> running in parallel, but the top rung of a validation ladder every hard
> case can climb. Second — sequence it behind the benchmark ladder's first
> rungs (M6/CRM) only because those need no closure escalation to land; the
> closure library gets built as the separated-flow cases (F6: hump, periodic
> hills, duct) come online, which are exactly the flows where stock closures
> do fail and the corrections do have published fixes. That's where the
> ladder earns its first certified "correction X recovers this flow where SST
> could not" — and that result is a genuine wall-of-credentials entry no
> competitor will have.
