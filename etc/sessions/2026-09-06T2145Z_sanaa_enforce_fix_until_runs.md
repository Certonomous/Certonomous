# SANAA-DIRECT — the fix-until-runs standard is ENFORCED, not doctrine (2026-09-06, ~21:45Z)

Captured verbatim from the chief session. This makes the mandatory-completion
law (4ae4b33, and the 09-04 orders) an executable check rather than a standard
living on trust — the root-cause fix the chief named for why the standard drifted.

## Sanaa's words, verbatim

> yes so i want it to be enforced. Now and going forward

(In answer to the chief's offer: make "fix until it runs, or prove it is a
genuine OpenFOAM capability gap" a STANDING ENFORCED CHECK — flagging any
GATE FAIL / NOT A RESULT that is neither proven a capability gap nor carrying
an active dated fix-successor — rather than a doctrine the chief has to police.)

## Context (chief's reading, not her words)

- **Root cause being fixed**: a written standard with no enforcing instrument
  behaves like a suggestion. §28's own repeated lesson about *code* — an
  invariant with no executable enforcer is not an invariant — is now applied
  to a *doctrine*. "Fix until it runs" had no standing check the way a freeze
  or a gate does, so it degraded to "fix when you get to it." That ends.
- **What the check must do** (verification designs the exact form under its
  own charter authority; this is the requirement, not the implementation):
  a standing instrument that enumerates every landed GATE FAIL / NOT A RESULT
  across all teams and flags any that is NOT in one of two acceptable states:
  (a) **proven a genuine OpenFOAM capability gap** — unrecoverability MEASURED
  and recorded, with bug / solver-selection / preconditioner / numerics-scheme
  / config / model-form each explicitly ruled out (the §2an five-process-class
  rule-out plus model-form via the closure ladder), filed on Sanaa's desk; or
  (b) **carrying an ACTIVE, DATED fix-successor** — a registered next attempt
  (successor registration or closure-ladder rung) that changes what failed and
  re-runs. Anything in neither state is a STANDING VIOLATION surfaced to the
  owning team and the chief.
- **"Now and going forward"**: it runs against the EXISTING backlog (the
  ~35-row ansys set, the T-family NOT-A-RESULTs, the closure/cfd/dafoam fails)
  AND on every future fail. The existing backlog's per-case five-point
  rule-out sweep is the first discharge of it.
- **Boundaries preserved**: the check FLAGS; it never widens a gate, never
  composes a verdict, never converts a NOT A RESULT to a pass. A fail is
  cleared only by a real fix that makes the case RUN, or by a measured
  capability-gap filing — never by the check's own action. Inbound research
  (papers, docs, OpenFOAM repos/issues) is the mandated fix method; outbound
  contact stays PARKED (rule 7). It is REPORTED/enforced per the governance
  posture, and being compute-protective and completeness-enforcing it is
  exactly the class of instrument that IS warranted (it changes whether the
  lab's own law is obeyed).
- **This is a charter-level standard**; verification lands the clause and the
  instrument, and whether any part touches CLAUDE.md itself is Sanaa's — the
  clause is written to live in the charters, not the constitution, unless she
  rules otherwise.
