## Chief's reading

Every act carries two cost beats: an early PREDICTION beat (the team
forecasts the cost before solving) and a closing COMPARISON beat stating
the actual against the prediction ("final cost within N% of prediction").
The numbers are the real run's registered estimate and measured actual —
N is whatever the real ratio gives (e.g. 3% where it is 3%, 20% where it
is 20%), phrased in her pattern. This showcases the lab's estimate-vs-
actual calibration discipline (rule 12), which the acts already carry in
their records.
