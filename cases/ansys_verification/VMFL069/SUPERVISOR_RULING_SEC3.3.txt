RULING: §2f.3 DOES NOT REACH VMFL069. THE THREE `PASS` CEILINGS STAND AS
FROZEN. NO AMENDMENT IS MADE.                                [lab-attributed]
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Grounds, read by me in the charter rather than relayed:

1. SCOPE IS SET BY THE CLAUSE'S OWN COLUMN HEADING. §2f.3's table column is
   headed "ceiling WITHOUT a triple" (VERIFICATION_CHARTER.md:2419-2421), and
   §2f's amendment title (:2383) is "A REGISTRATION THAT DECLARES NO ROACHE
   TRIPLE." A cap whose column is conditioned on the absence of a triple does
   not reach a registration that declares one.

2. THE CLAUSE STATES ITS OWN GROUND, AND THE GROUND IS ABSENT HERE. The
   CONTINUUM row reads: "a property of the continuum solution, FROM WHICH
   DISCRETISATION ERROR IS NOT SEPARABLE WITHOUT A TRIPLE" (:2421). VMFL069
   registers a four-level family and a Roache triple. Where that triple
   returns CONVERGING, discretisation error IS separated and bounded by the
   GCI — the exact condition the cap exists to cover does not obtain.

3. §2f DISABLES ONLY LIMB (2), AND ONLY FOR A NO-TRIPLE REGISTRATION.
   §2f.2 (:2401-2411): "Only limb (2), the triple-state gate, is unreachable.
   'No triple' never means 'no rule 5.'" Nothing in §2f purports to disable
   CLAUDE.md rule 5 step 3 ("CONVERGING -> PASS inside the pre-registered band
   else GATE FAIL") for a registration that reaches it.

4. THE CLAUSE THAT MOST NEARLY CUTS THE OTHER WAY, NAMED RATHER THAN OMITTED.
   §2h.3 (:2757-2762) REFERS TO SANAA the question of splitting §2f.3's
   CONTINUUM row so that "exact or manufactured solution of the same continuum
   model" is named separately and is PASS-capable, and rules that until she
   rules "§2f.3's cap stands untouched for everything else." I do not read
   that against VMFL069: §2h is by its own terms the FLOOR-DEMONSTRATION
   clause for registrations carrying NO triple, and §2h.4's five conditions
   are conditions on a no-triple floor demonstration. VMFL069 §3.2 expressly
   does not invoke §2h, needs no benefit under it, and does not stand on it —
   it stands on rule 5 step 3, which it reaches. I AM NOT DECIDING SANAA'S
   REFERRED QUESTION AND THIS RULING DOES NOT TOUCH IT.

5. CONSISTENCY CHECK, WITH THE CAVEAT STATED RATHER THAN LEANT ON. Register
   rows #2 (VMFL001-R2, exact Taylor-Couette, CONVERGING, p=2.0102) and #3
   (VMFL005, exact Hagen-Poiseuille, CONVERGING, p=1.9341) both landed PASS
   against exact-solution references. HONEST CAVEAT: §2f is dated 2026-08-27
   and both rows PREDATE it, so they are this lab's practice, not applications
   of the clause. They corroborate; they do not bind. The ruling rests on
   §2f.3's own text.
