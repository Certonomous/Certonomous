  MAY  -- exercise the toolchain: mesh, patches, dictionaries, launcher abort

  MAY NOT -- produce a converged solve or a gate value.
The smoke below runs L1 for THREE time steps. The slowest mode of the discrete
operator has a time constant of 33.4 s and the registered run is 2000 steps, so
three steps is about 1/600 of one time constant and roughly 1/670 of the run. It
writes no time directory at all (writeInterval is 500), so there is no field on
disk from which a gate quantity could have been taken, and none was taken. The
smoke ran ONLY in a scratch root; the launcher refuses any other.
