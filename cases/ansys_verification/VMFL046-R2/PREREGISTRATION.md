## 12. THE PLANTED CONTROL (rule 3) — **DESIGNED SO IT CAN FAIL, AND PROVED TO FAIL**

> ### THE RULE THIS CONTROL IS BUILT TO — adopted for this territory by the supervisor, 2026-09-04
>
> **A PLANTED CONTROL MUST BE DESIGNED AGAINST THE *REDUCTION*, NOT AGAINST THE FIELD.**
> For a **mean**, plant a proper subset. For a **peak-to-peak**, plant the current **extremum**.
> For a **max**, plant the **argmax**. **A plant that does not move the specific statistic the gate
> reads is INERT no matter how large it is.**

> The rule was paid for twice in two days, by two different failure modes of the same mistake:

> identically unchanged (last night's dead control); and **ABSORPTION** — a plant into an *interior*
> member is swallowed whole when the window already spreads wider than the plant (measured below,
> on this case's own real data). Both are instances of designing against the field instead of
> against the statistic.
