PURPOSE: confirm the ACTUAL on-disk names, shapes and column orders the function

CLEANLY FROM THE FROZEN case/ templates (materialised exactly as run_vmflgpu005.sh

CASE RAN: solver rc=0, 5 Time lines, End present, checkMesh cells 6720 (== the L1

omega and k, and the run reached endTime.

TWO DEFECTS CAUGHT AND REPAIRED BEFORE FREEZE (this is what the smoke is for):

  DEFECT 1 -- ALPHABETICAL FIELD ORDER. The `sets` sampler writes fields SORTED
  ALPHABETICALLY, so the file is `<set>_T_U.xy` with columns  x  T  Ux Uy Uz  --
  T BEFORE U. The comparator's first draft assumed x,Ux,Uy,Uz,T and would have
  read the TEMPERATURE column as Uy. Column constants corrected to
  COL_X=0, COL_T=1, COL_UX=2, COL_UY=3, COL_UZ=4 and driven on the real file.

  DEFECT 2 -- wallHeatFlux DOES NOT WORK FOR THE BOUSSINESQ SOLVER. The
  `wallHeatFlux` FO refused at execute: "Unable to find compressible turbulence
  model in the database" (buoyantBoussinesqSimpleFoam carries no compressible
  thermo). The Nu channel was re-based, PRE-FREEZE, on the WALL-NORMAL TEMPERATURE
  GRADIENT: grad(T) is materialised as a volume field and surface-sampled on the
  walls, and Nu_hot = |grad(T)_x|_hot_avg * W / dT_wall (on the resolved integrate-
  to-wall mesh the wall flux is conductive; alphat -> 0 at the wall, so K_AIR
  cancels). Confirmed emitting: postProcessing/wallGradT/5/grad(T)_hotWall.raw.
