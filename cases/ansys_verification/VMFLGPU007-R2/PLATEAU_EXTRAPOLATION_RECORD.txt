(b) THE EXTRAPOLATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For a geometric increment sequence with ratio r = exp(-1/tau), the W-sample
peak-to-peak is ptp(n) = d(n-W) * (1 - r^W)/(1 - r), so
      ptp(n + D) / ptp(n) = exp(-D/tau)   EXACTLY.
Hence D = tau * ln(ptp_now / TOL), plus a margin of 3 tau (a factor e^3 =
20.1 of headroom on the peak-to-peak), rounded UP to the next multiple of 200.
The CONSERVATIVE reading is used at every level: the LARGER of the two arms'
peak-to-peak and the LARGEST tau over the three fit windows and both arms.

HONEST STATEMENT OF CONFIDENCE.  The fit quality is the evidence: R^2 = 0.9999
to 1.0000 on the last 400 increments of every arm, tau agreeing to within 4 %
across window widths and to within 1 % between the two arms.  A 10 % error in
tau moves the required extra iterations by 10 % of ~1300-1700, i.e. 130-170,
which the 3-tau margin (691-1432 iterations) absorbs several times over.

THE ONE THING THAT COULD FALSIFY THIS, NAMED RATHER THAN GLOSSED.  If a SLOWER
secondary mode exists below the current increment level, the single-exponential
extrapolation is optimistic.  There is a real candidate: the p_rgh and epsilon
initial residuals have STALLED.  Measured on the gpu arms:

  L1   3.73e-6 -> 3.22e-6 over the last 240 its    8.67e-7 -> 6.04e-7

while h_initial (the ENERGY equation, which is what drives wallTmin) is still

THE EVIDENCE THAT THE STALL DOES NOT CONTAMINATE THE CHANNEL IS L3 ITSELF:

stretch wallTmin's increments fit a single exponential with R^2 = 1.000000 and
a 200-sample noise floor of 2.1e-7 K -- the LOWEST of the six arms.  A stalled
pressure residual at ~1e-5 is not visibly perturbing this channel above 1e-6 K.
I flag it as the residual risk and do not claim it is eliminated.
