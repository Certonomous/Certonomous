CAUSE. grade_vmfl063.py:867 opens its selftest sandbox with
`tempfile.mkdtemp(prefix="vmfl063_selftest_")`, and 20 of the selftest's 78
output lines quote that absolute path inside their refusal messages (the
cardinality-guard, completion, age-guard, time-directory and planted-zero
refusals all name the file they refused on -- which is good diagnostics). The
suffix is random per process, so two runs never agree byte-for-byte. Sites 420
and 473 use mkdtemp identically for the two plant channels.

WHAT IS AND IS NOT WRONG:
  - The COMPARATOR is not defective. Its -O-invariance is real: with the one
    nondeterministic token normalised, the two interpreters produce identical
    output, and both give 62/62 with zero asserts. L-332 is satisfied in
    substance. My own independent ast walk of the source confirms
    ast.Assert = 0, ast.Raise = 43.
  - The LAUNCHER's check is defective: `cmp` is the wrong instrument for an
    output that legitimately carries a per-process path. The check intends
    "the controls do not change under -O" and instead tests "the output has no
    nondeterministic token", which no run can satisfy.
  - PREREGISTRATION.md sec.8 states the selftest is "BYTE-IDENTICAL output
    under python3 and python3 -O". As literally written that is FALSE and is
    now measured false. In substance -- the controls exercised, their counts,
    their verdicts and their text modulo the sandbox path -- it is TRUE.
    RECORDED, NOT CORRECTED: the pre-registration is frozen and this lane does
    not edit it. This paragraph is a referral, not an amendment.
