# PREREGISTRATION - Ling, Kurzawski & Templeton (2016) TBNN, as a labelled VARIANT

Written **before** any training. Nothing in this file may be edited after the
first training run; corrections go in `RESULTS.md` as amendments with a reason.

Date written: 2026-08-20.

## 1. The paper and its number

**Source status: VERIFIED-PDF.** `docs/papers/closure/Ling2016_tbnn_embedded_invariance.pdf`,
printed title page reads `SAND2016-7345J`, *Reynolds Averaged Turbulence Modeling
using Deep Neural Networks with Embedded Invariance*, Julia Ling and Jeremy
Templeton (Sandia), Andrew Kurzawski (UT Austin), dated July 24 2016. Published as
J. Fluid Mech. 807:155-166.

**Their headline number, Table I, p. 11 - RMSE of the anisotropy tensor `b_ij`
against DNS on two held-out flows:**

| Model | Duct flow, `Re_b` = 2000 | Wavy wall, `Re` = 6850 |
|---|---|---|
| LEVM (linear eddy viscosity) | 0.23 | 0.18 |
| QEVM (quadratic) | 0.18 | 0.11 |
| **TBNN** | **0.13** | **0.08** |
| plain MLP | 0.33 | 0.09 |

Their stated improvements: "43% more accurate than the LEVM and 28% more accurate
than the QEVM" on the duct (p. 10) and "a 56% reduction in error with respect to
LEVM and a 27% reduction in error with respect to QEVM" on the wavy wall (p. 11).

**Their architecture, p. 9:** input layer = the 5 invariants `lambda_1..lambda_5`;
8 hidden layers of 30 nodes each, chosen by Bayesian optimisation; a Final Hidden
Layer of 10 elements representing `g^(n)`, `n = 1..10`; a Tensor Input Layer of
the 10 basis tensors `T^(n)`; a Merge Output Layer performing element-wise
multiplication and summation. Their comparison MLP: 10 hidden layers x 10 nodes,
learning rate 2.5e-6 (p. 6).

**Their data, p. 8:** train on 6 flows (duct `Re_b`=3500; channel `Re_tau`=590;
perpendicular jet in crossflow; inclined jet in crossflow; square cylinder;
converging-diverging channel); validate on a wall-mounted cube in crossflow at
`Re_b`=5000; test on duct `Re_b`=2000 and wavy wall `Re`=6850.

## 2. THIS IS A VARIANT. The departure, stated up front

**Not one of their nine flows is on this machine, and their database is not
distributed as a single archive.** We therefore reproduce the *method* on a
different flow set: the Closure Challenge benchmark (`BASELINES.md` sec. 6).

Consequences, all of which are disclosed again in `RESULTS.md`:

* **The number 0.13 is not a target we can hit or miss.** Different flows,
  different `Re`, different DNS/LES sources, different meshes. Any comparison of
  our RMSE to 0.13 would be meaningless and will not be made.
* What *is* testable is the paper's **structural claim**: that embedding the
  tensor basis makes the network beat (a) a linear eddy-viscosity model and
  (b) an unconstrained MLP of comparable capacity on held-out flows. That claim
  is flow-independent and is what this reproduction tests.
* Their invariants use `k/eps` as the time scale. Our benchmark ships `omega`,
  so `eps = beta* k omega` with `beta* = 0.09` [VERIFIED-PDF: Menter 1994,
  eq. (A6), p. 1603]. Stated as a departure because it introduces the SST
  `beta*` into the normalisation.

## 3. What is being predicted, and from what

* **Target:** `b_ij = tau_ij/(2k) - delta_ij/3` built from the shipped
  `tauij_LES` and `k_LES`, i.e. the same target `sst_baseline_metrics.py` scores.
* **Inputs:** `lambda_1 = tr(s^2)`, `lambda_2 = tr(r^2)`, `lambda_3 = tr(s^3)`,
  `lambda_4 = tr(r^2 s)`, `lambda_5 = tr(r^2 s^2)`, with
  `s = S k/eps`, `r = W k/eps`, `S` and `W` the symmetric and antisymmetric
  parts of `dU_i/dx_j` from the **RANS** field only.
* **Basis:** `T^(1..10)` exactly as Pope 1975 p. 335 [VERIFIED-PDF] and as
  Ling 2016 eq. (5).
* Nothing from the LES field is used as an input. Only `b_LES` is a label.

## 4. Trivial baselines that must be beaten

From `BASELINES.md` (generated by `sst_baseline_metrics.py`, re-run 2026-08-20
and byte-identical, md5 `4fc917f2300bcada2fa6eb25f454e9b5`):

| Baseline | Value on the 8 strict TEST cases |
|---|---|
| **B1: k-omega SST itself** (`b_rms` = RMS of `\|\|b_RANS - b_LES\|\|_F`) | hills `alpha_05_4071_2024` 0.3202, `alpha_05_4071_4048` 0.3498, `alpha_15_13929_2024` 0.2989, `alpha_15_13929_4048` 0.2885; ducts `AR_1_Ret_360` 0.5843, `AR_3_Ret_360` 0.5431, `AR_14_Ret_180` 0.5767; `NASA_2DWMH` 0.3196 |
| **B2: the isotropic predictor** `b = 0` | computed at run time as RMS of `\|\|b_LES\|\|_F`. Any model that cannot beat *predicting zero anisotropy* has learned nothing |
| **B3: the training-set mean predictor** `b = mean(b_LES over training cells)` | computed at run time |
| **B4: an unconstrained MLP** of comparable parameter count taking the same 5 invariants and regressing the 6 independent components of `b` directly | trained here, same budget, same seeds. This is Ling's own control and it is the one that tests the *invariance embedding* rather than the flows |

## 5. Acceptance band and falsifying outcomes

Declared before training:

* **PASS** requires *all* of: (i) TBNN test `b_rms` **below B1 (SST) on at least
  6 of the 8 TEST cases**; (ii) TBNN test `b_rms` below B2 and B3 on at least 6
  of 8; (iii) TBNN mean test `b_rms` **below the MLP's** (B4) by more than the
  seed spread (defined as the max-minus-min over 5 seeds).
* **GATE REACHED** if (i) and (ii) hold but (iii) does not - i.e. the method
  beats the physics baselines but the invariance embedding is not shown to be
  the reason.
* **GATE FAIL** if the TBNN beats SST on 4 or 5 of 8 cases.
* **NOT A RESULT** if the TBNN beats SST on 3 or fewer of 8, or if it fails to
  beat B2 (`b = 0`) anywhere.
* **Explicit falsifier:** if the plain MLP (B4) equals or beats the TBNN on the
  held-out ducts, the paper's central claim - that embedding Galilean invariance
  through the tensor basis improves generalisation - is **not** reproduced on
  this data, and that is the finding, reported as such.

## 6. Split, and the disjointness assertion

The benchmark's **strict** rule is that no TEST case may be trained or validated
on. Beyond that, `BASELINES.md` sec. 6.2 warns that the 29 hills are not 29
independent flows: they come in triples sharing (`alpha`, domain length) and
differing only in domain height, so a naive split leaks near-duplicates.

* **TEST (never seen, 8 cases):** `alpha_15_13929_4048`, `alpha_15_13929_2024`,
  `alpha_05_4071_4048`, `alpha_05_4071_2024`, `AR_1_Ret_360`, `AR_3_Ret_360`,
  `AR_14_Ret_180`, `NASA_2DWMH`.
* **VALIDATION (5 cases):** `alpha_05_10071_4048`, `alpha_05_10071_2024`,
  `alpha_15_7929_4048`, `alpha_15_7929_2024`, `AR_7_Ret_180`.
* **TRAIN:** everything else - 20 hills, 4 ducts (`AR_1/3/5/10_Ret_180`),
  `PHLL10595`, `CBFS13700`.
* **Additional group rule:** the whole (`alpha`, length) triple of every TEST and
  VALIDATION hill is excluded from training. Concretely `alpha_15_13929_3036`
  and `alpha_05_4071_3036` are **removed from training** even though the
  benchmark permits them, because they differ from a TEST case only in domain
  height. This costs 2 training cases and removes the leak.
* **Assertion in code:** `assert_disjoint()` verifies that the sets of case
  names are pairwise disjoint, that no `(alpha, length)` group appears in both
  train and TEST/VAL, and that the cell-index sets are disjoint by construction
  (cases are whole units; no cell is split). The assertion raises before any
  training step. **Its output is printed into `RESULTS.md` verbatim.**

## 7. Seeds, and what is averaged

Five seeds: `0, 1, 2, 3, 4`. Each seed re-initialises the network weights and
re-shuffles minibatches; the data split is **fixed** and does not depend on the
seed. Reported: mean and full range (max - min) of the test `b_rms` over the 5
seeds, per case. A difference smaller than the seed range is reported as no
difference.

## 8. Checks that will be reported whatever the outcome

1. **Realisability of the predicted `b`**: fraction of test cells whose predicted
   `b` falls outside the barycentric triangle, using
   `_common/of_read.py:realisability_violation()`. Reported **next to the truth's
   own violation fraction** (1.2-2.5% on the hills, up to 1.6% on the ducts -
   `BASELINES.md` sec. 5), never next to zero.
2. **Extrapolation statistic**: Mahalanobis distance of each test cell's 5
   invariants from the training-set invariant distribution, with the fraction of
   test cells beyond the 99th percentile of the training distribution.
3. **Continuity check**: **not applicable** - this reproduction produces no
   velocity field.
4. **A-posteriori note**: this run is **a-priori only**. The predicted `b` is
   **not** re-solved in a momentum equation. OpenFOAM v2606 is available on this
   machine, so the absence of an a-posteriori result is a scope decision, not a
   capability limit, and it will be stated in exactly those words.

## 9. Compute

Estimated well under 1 core-hour total for 5 seeds x (TBNN + MLP). Bounded by a
hard cap of 400 epochs and a wall-clock self-timeout; checkpoints written every
25 epochs so a restart resumes rather than repeats.

## 11. Failure disposition

A failed reproduction ships as a documented failure with the verdict from the
fixed vocabulary {PASS, GATE REACHED, GATE FAIL, NOT A RESULT, BLOCKED, PENDING}.
It is not retried with different hyper-parameters into a pass. If a genuine bug
is found, the fix is recorded as an amendment in `RESULTS.md` naming the bug, and
the pre-registered acceptance band is not moved.
