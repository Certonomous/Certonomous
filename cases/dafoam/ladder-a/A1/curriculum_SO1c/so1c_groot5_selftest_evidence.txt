registered 0/7.  Triage: the driver's embedded G-SO1B python carried a LITERAL
NEWLINE inside a string literal --

and the board would have read that as a registered no-launch close rather than as
an instrument defect.  Repaired before the freeze; the driver md5 was re-derived
and re-pinned in PREREGISTRATION.md section 7 afterwards.
